# Pecun partner posts

One post per vault provider live on Pecun: attach the graphic from `graphics/` and post the text.

- Handles marked "check" weren't found on the protocol's own site: open their X profile and confirm before posting.
- These say "live on Pecun", not "partnership": only call it a partnership once they've agreed to one.
- Space them out: 2 or 3 a day reads as momentum, 29 in a day reads as spam.
