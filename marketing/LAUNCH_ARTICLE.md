There's more than $64 billion sitting in onchain yield vaults right now: lending markets, staking, fixed rates and strategies run by some of the sharpest risk teams in crypto. It's the closest thing the internet has to an open savings market, open to anyone with a wallet. And yet most people will never touch it, not because they don't want the yield, but because getting to it is a job.

## The part nobody puts on the dashboard

Say you hold some USDC and you spot a vault paying a rate you like. It's on Arbitrum, or Solana, or a chain you've never opened before. So you go looking for a bridge and hope the one you pick isn't quietly expensive. When the money lands, you can't move it, because you don't hold that chain's gas token, so you buy some of that too. Then you swap into whatever the vault actually takes, approve it, deposit it, and try to remember where everything ended up. Months later, when you want out, you do it all again in reverse.

Every one of those steps costs something: a fee here, a bad rate there, twenty minutes of second-guessing. On a small deposit, a careless route can eat 2% just getting in, which is more than a month of yield gone before you've earned anything. So most people don't bother, and the money sits in a wallet doing nothing. That's the real gap in DeFi. The yield is there; the road to it isn't.

## So we built the road

Pecun puts every yield onchain one click away. You keep USDC on Base, pick any vault on any chain we support, and press once.

Behind that one press, Pecun does the work you used to do by hand. It compares several bridges and routes at once (Relay, LI.FI, Across and the networks they reach) and takes whichever gets the most of your money to the other side. A small part arrives as that chain's own coin, so your transactions there go through without you ever buying a gas token, and the rest is swapped into exactly what the vault takes and deposited. Your position lands in your own wallet, and when you want out, the same press brings it straight back to USDC on Base.

Today that covers more than 1,600 vaults from over 30 protocols across more than 20 chains: Aave and Morpho lending, Pendle fixed rates, Lido staking, Kamino on Solana, Beefy's auto-compounders, Ethena, Spark, Euler, Fluid, Ember, Upshift and many more, all in one list, sorted by rate.

## Built to protect what you earn

Moving money across chains has hidden costs, so Pecun puts all of them in front of you. Before you press, you see Pecun's 0.5% fee alongside the bridge and swap costs, and what you'll end up with in the vault. Every route is held to a limit too: if moving your money in or out of a vault would cost more than 0.5%, Pecun won't run it, and vaults that can't meet that standard aren't listed at all. The fee is only taken once your deposit has landed. If a transfer fails part-way, nothing is charged and your funds are returned to your wallet automatically.

## Self-custodial

Pecun is self-custodial. Your positions sit in your own wallet, and only you can move them. Your keys are secured by @turnkeyhq, and you sign in with your email or a passkey. No seed phrase, no extension.

## All of it, in one place

Because your positions live onchain, Pecun reads them straight from the chains: every vault you're in, on every network, with what you put in, what it's worth now and what it's earned. Sign in on your phone or your laptop and it's the same picture.

## Who this is for

If you've ever looked at a rate on another chain and decided it wasn't worth the hassle, or stayed away from DeFi because it looked like a maze, Pecun is for you. And if you've been in DeFi for years and you're tired of doing the routing yourself, it's for you too.

Getting started takes a minute: sign in with your email or a passkey, send some USDC on Base to your Pecun wallet, pick a vault and press once. Everything about how it works, including the risks, is at **docs.pecun.finance**.

Put your money to work.
