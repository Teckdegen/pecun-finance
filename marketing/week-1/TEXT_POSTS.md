## Hot takes, part 3

```text
TVL isn't a safety score.

A vault with $1B in it can still have a bug, a bad oracle or a risky curator. Size tells you others trust it, not that it's safe.

Read what a vault does and how you exit. Pecun puts both on every vault page, one click from depositing.
```

```text
A stablecoin is only as stable as what backs it.

Cash and T-bills, other crypto, or an algorithm: three very different risks wearing the same $1 price tag. Depegs happen.

Know which one you hold before you chase its yield. Pecun shows the asset on every vault.
```

```text
An APY paid in a reward token is two bets, not one.

The rate, and the price of the token it's paid in. If that token halves, so does that part of your yield.

Pecun splits out reward APY where the protocol publishes it, so you can see which part is which.
```

```text
Liquidity isn't free money. It's getting paid to take the other side.

LPs earn fees because traders need someone to trade against. When prices move hard, LPs can end up with more of the falling asset.

Know the trade before you take it. Pools are one click away on Pecun.
```

```text
"Withdraw any time" has fine print in DeFi.

A lending market can run out of cash when everyone borrows at once. A vault can queue exits. A fixed-rate PT pays full value only at maturity.

The exit is part of the product. Pecun shows it on every vault before you go in.
```

```text
The fee you see isn't the cost you pay.

Every route has a bridge fee, swap fees and price impact on top of any app fee, and you pay them twice: in and out.

Pecun shows the whole round trip before you press, and won't run a route that costs over 0.5% on top of its fee.
```

```text
Small deposits get punished by fixed costs.

A $2 bridge fee is 0.02% of $10,000 and 2% of $100. Same fee, 100x the damage.

That's why Pecun checked every route at $100 and only lists vaults where the trip in and out stays under 0.5%.
```

```text
"Not your keys" was never just about exchanges.

It's about who can move your money. Your Pecun wallet's keys sit in secure hardware, and only your own sign-in can approve a move. Our servers can't do it alone.
```

```text
A failed transaction shouldn't cost you anything.

In most DeFi flows, if step 4 of 5 breaks you've already paid fees and your money is stuck on a chain you didn't want.

On Pecun, the fee is only taken once your deposit lands, and anything that moved comes back to your wallet on its own.
```

```text
Being on Robinhood Chain shouldn't cap what you can earn.

Your money can live on one chain and still work on twenty. Hold USDG at home, earn wherever the best vault is, and every withdrawal comes back to USDG.

That's the whole idea of Pecun.
```

```text
The best rate is rarely on the chain you're on.

Rates move between chains every week as borrowing demand shifts. Loyalty to one chain is a tax on your yield.

Pecun shows 1,600+ vaults across 20+ chains in one list, each one click away.
```

```text
No legit protocol will ask for your login code, your passkey, or for you to "send funds to verify." Pecun never will.

Token contracts only come from official docs and official accounts. Anything else is a trap.
```
